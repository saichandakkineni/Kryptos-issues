Privacy Policy — Kryptos

Effective date: May 22, 2026 · Last updated: May 27, 2026

App: Kryptos (iOS) · Publisher: Kryptos · Contact: kryptos.enquiry@gmail.com · Support: Kryptos Support

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Kryptos handles information when you use our iOS password manager. Kryptos is designed to be privacy-first: your master password and decrypted vault contents stay on your device. We do not operate a Kryptos cloud service that receives your passwords.

2. Summary

Topic Our approach
Your passwords Encrypted on your device; we never receive your master password
Cloud sync Optional; only an encrypted vault file in your iCloud or Google Drive
Analytics & ads None
Selling data We do not sell your personal information

3. Information you provide

You choose what to store in your vault: logins, secure notes, cards, identity documents, Wi‑Fi passwords, SSH keys, passkeys, and related metadata. This is used only to provide app functionality.

4. Storage and protection

Vault data is encrypted on device with AES-256-GCM. Keys use Argon2id. Your master password is never transmitted to us. Biometric data never leaves your device (Apple LocalAuthentication).

5. Optional cloud sync

Sync stores an encrypted Kryptos.vault file in your iCloud Drive or Google Drive. Kryptos cannot decrypt your cloud file without your master password.

6. Network use

Feature Data sent
Breach check First 5 chars of SHA-1 hash (k-anonymity) to Have I Been Pwned
Favicons Website domain to Google favicon service
iCloud / Google sync Encrypted vault blob via Apple or Google APIs

7. Permissions

Face ID / Touch ID, Camera (TOTP QR), and Photo Library (identity attachments) when you use those features.

8. What we do not collect

No advertising identifiers, cross-app tracking, analytics sold to brokers, or your master password on our servers.

9. Children’s privacy

Kryptos is not directed at children under 13. Contact kryptos.enquiry@gmail.com or use our support page if you have concerns.

10. Your rights

View, edit, export, and delete data in the app. Revoke cloud sync in Settings. Contact us for privacy requests at kryptos.enquiry@gmail.com.

We may update this policy; the dates at the top will change when we do.
